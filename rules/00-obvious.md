# Do the Obvious

Everyone knows what to do. The problem is execution.
Stop overthinking and just do the basics properly.

Everything written in these rules is mostly obvious.
The reason we write it down is because the obvious doesn't happen obviously.
Knowing and not doing is worse than not knowing at all.

## Aim for the Best, Not the Average (2026-08-06)

- Think against the BEST, not the average. "Standard practice" is what average
  garbage looks like; judge every choice by "최고의 엔지니어라면 어떻게 했을까?" —
  and ask it BEFORE acting, not after being corrected.
- NO GENERIC DEFAULTS. Every parameter, estimator, guard, and figure choice is
  DERIVED from this problem's instrument, physics, and audience — or it does
  not ship.
- "적당히 잘 맞겠지" IS FORBIDDEN. Either the choice is derived and defensible,
  or it is checked against data, or it is asked. No third option.
- Whatever the work is — methodology, code, a figure, a log entry — do it with
  CARE (섬세하게). Never roughly. Precision in the small things is what "best"
  means in practice.
